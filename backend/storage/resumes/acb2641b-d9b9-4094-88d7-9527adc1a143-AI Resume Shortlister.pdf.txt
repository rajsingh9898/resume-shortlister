Problem Statement
The Challenge
•Companies  and recruiters  still evaluate  candidates  manually  by reading  resumes  one by one and 
comparing  them  against  job descriptions  — this takes  significant  time and often  leads  to 
inconsistent  hiring  decisions  and missed  qualified  candidates .
•Candidate  information  and skill requirements  are scattered  across  multiple  resume  formats  (PDF,  
DOCX,  TXT)  and job description  documents,  making  it difficult  for hiring  teams  to quickly  identify  
top matches  and create  accurate  evaluation  reports .
•Small  and medium -sized  companies  usually  don't  have  access  to expensive  enterprise  recruiting  
software  where  candidate  evaluations,  scoring,  and team  feedback  can be managed  in one 
centralized  location .
3 / 25

Problem Statement
Our Solution
•TalentAI  is an AI-powered  recruitment  platform  that automates  resume  parsing,  skill matching,  
candidate  scoring,  and shortlisting .
•It uses hybrid  NLP techniques,  combining  semantic  embeddings  and keyword  matching  to evaluate  
candidates  against  job requirements .
•It provides  AI-generated  insights,  analytics  dashboards,  candidate  evaluation  tools,  and PDF reports  
to support  faster  and data-driven  hiring  decisions .

Project Introduction
•Project Name:  TalentAI
•Project Type:  AI-Powered Resume Screening & Candidate Ranking System
•Purpose:  To automate resume screening, candidate evaluation, and shortlisting .
•Provides a centralized platform for resume analysis and candidate matching .
•Uses NLP, semantic embeddings, and keyword matching  to evaluate candidates.
•Developed using Python, FastAPI, Sentence -Transformers, and Scikit -learn .
•Provides candidate scoring, AI insights, dashboards, and reports .
•Focuses on faster, accurate, and data -driven recruitment .

•User  Authentication  & Upload  – Recruiter  logs in and uploads  resumes  and job descriptions  
through  the frontend . 
•Document  Processing  – FastAPI  processes  PDF,  DOCX,  and TXT  files and extracts  relevant  
candidate  information . 
•NLP  & Skill  Matching  – The system  uses Sentence -Transformers  and TF-IDF to extract  skills  
and match  candidates  with job requirements . 
•Scoring  & AI Evaluation  – Candidates  are scored  and ranked , with AI-generated  verdicts,  
pros/cons,  and interview  questions . 
•Results  & Decision  – Scores  and analytics  are stored  in the database  and displayed  through

Database
•Database : PostgreSQL  (with  SQLite  fallback) .
•PostgreSQL  is used as the primary  database,  with SQLite  automatically  used  when  PostgreSQL  is 
unavailable . 
•The database  manages  candidate  data,  evaluation  information,  scores,  recruiter  notes,  and 
candidate  statuses .
•Alembic  is used for database  migrations  and table  initialization . 
API(Application Programming Interface)
•REST  APIs  are used for communication  between  the frontend  and FastAPI  backend .
•FastAPI  is used to build  the backend  server  and REST  endpoints . 
•The backend  handles  resume  processing,  candidate  scoring,  evaluation,  and related  
recruitment  operations .

Conclusion
